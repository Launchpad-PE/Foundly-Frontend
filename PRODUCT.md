# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and young professionals who either need collaborators for their projects or want to contribute their skills to other people's projects. They use Foundly on desktop primarily, in an exploratory or task-completing mindset: browsing projects, reviewing profiles, posting opportunities, and managing their participation.

## Product Purpose

Foundly connects people around projects. It lets anyone publish a project and find collaborators with the right skills, or discover projects worth joining and apply. Success means a user either finds the right team member or lands a meaningful collaboration quickly.

## Brand Personality

Collaborative, dynamic, accessible. Warm without being casual. Confident without being corporate. The product should feel like a place where ambitious people come to build things together, not file paperwork or compete for gigs.

## Anti-references

- LinkedIn: too cold, corporate, hierarchical. Foundly is about building, not networking for status.
- GitHub: too developer-centric, dark, text-heavy. Foundly is for any skill set, not just engineers.

## Design Principles

1. **Show potential, not process.** Every screen should help the user imagine what they could build or who they could become. Avoid form-heavy, step-heavy bureaucratic flows.
2. **Accessible to non-technical users.** Avoid jargon, dark modes, or patterns that signal "for developers only."
3. **Momentum over completeness.** Make the key action on each screen obvious. Don't let secondary content compete with the primary call to action.
4. **People first.** Profiles and collaborators should feel as prominent as projects. This is a community, not a job board.
5. **Consistent violet identity.** The brand color (violet, approx #6c63ff) is the thread connecting every surface. Use it with restraint but not timidity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Body text must hit 4.5:1 contrast. Support reduced motion. The product spans multiple skill domains, so copy should be jargon-free and scannable.
